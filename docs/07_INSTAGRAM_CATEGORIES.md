# 07 · Instagram categories — what to search, follow and post against

You asked for the list of categories you search for and follow. I can't read your Instagram
account, so this is the **map of categories that matters for this suite**: the 30 trades you now
have demos for, the exact search terms that surface them on Instagram, and the hashtag families
that are actually used in Pakistan.

If you paste your "Following" list (Settings → Your activity → Interactions → or export from
Accounts Centre), I will re-cut this into *your* list, sorted by who is most likely to buy.

---

## 1 · How to use this

1. **Search the term, not the hashtag** — Instagram's search now ranks topics, and hashtags are
   half-dead. Search e.g. `dental clinic karachi`, then open the *Accounts* tab and follow the
   practices and the people who serve them.
2. **Follow three kinds of account per category:**
   - the **businesses** themselves (they are the buyers),
   - the **consultants/agencies** who already sell to them (partners and competitors),
   - the **suppliers** (equipment, software, distributors) — they comment on every post and are the
     cheapest source of warm introductions.
3. **Save one post per category** into a named collection (`Agency`, `Dental`, `Poultry`…). That
   becomes your content swipe file: the hook, the visual, the CTA.

## 3 · The order I would work through them

Ranked by how easily a Rs 25–60K dashboard closes, not by how interesting the industry is.

| Priority | Categories | Why first |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Dental/clinic · Salon · Gym · Restaurant | Owners are on Instagram daily, decisions are one person, and the pain (no-shows, wastage, churn) is instantly legible |
| **2** | Fuel station · Pharmacy · Auto workshop · Bakery | Fixed margins, high transaction counts, and they *already* keep a register — the sheet is half-built |
| **3** | Real estate · Travel · Event photography | High ticket, so a dashboard is a rounding error; slower to reply though |
| **4** | Poultry · Garment · Printing · Manufacturing | Best margins per project, but you need Urdu/technical fluency and a site visit |
| **5** | Coaching · School · Logistics · Courier | Budget exists but procurement is slow |
| **6** | SaaS · MSP · Law · Architecture | They will often build it themselves — sell the template, not the service |

## 4 · What to actually post (per category)

One format, repeated: **the problem, the number, the screen.**

| Slide | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | The number that hurts, in the owner's units — "217 scripts a day, 20.9% margin, and Rs 90,455 of stock expiring in 90 days." |
| 2 | The same number as a screenshot from the matching demo. Crop to the KPI row. |
| 3 | One line: "Six numbers, one Google Sheet, no new software." + `DM 'demo'` |

Reel version: 12 seconds of scrolling the live dashboard on a phone, captioned with the industry's
own vocabulary. Do **not** narrate features; narrate the loss.

## 5 · Relationship list to build alongside the follows

For each of the 30 categories, follow these three *types* — this is the part most people skip:

1. **The trade association / directory account** (e.g. a local dental association, a poultry
   association chapter, a printers' guild). They announce members, events and price changes — free
   intelligence.
2. **The equipment or software vendor** in that vertical (dental chair importers, POS vendors,
   poultry feed mills). Their comment sections are full of owners asking operational questions.
3. **The one consultant who already serves that niche.** Not to compete — to partner. A marketing
   consultant with twelve dental clients is a better first customer than any single clinic.

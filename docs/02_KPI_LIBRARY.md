# 02 · KPI Library — what each industry watches, and why

The rule used across all fifteen: **six numbers, three charts, one table.** If a metric can't
change a decision this week, it doesn't belong on the first screen. Vanity metrics
(impressions, likes, page views) were deliberately left off — that's the single most common
complaint owners have about agency dashboards.

Benchmarks below are published industry figures, so the demo numbers can be defended in a sales
call. Sources are linked at the bottom.

---

## 01 · Digital Marketing Agency
**Decision it drives:** which retainers are renewing, and who owes us money.

| KPI | Why it's there |
|---|---|
| Pipeline value | The real forward book, not "leads generated" |
| Active retainers | Signed recurring relationships |
| Monthly recurring (MRR) | The number owners actually run the business on |
| Collected this month | Cash in — not invoiced |
| Overdue receivables | The leak nobody watches until payroll |
| Follow-ups due (7d) | Activity that prevents the pipeline dying quietly |

Charts: 12-month collected revenue · lead source mix · pipeline by stage.
**Benchmark:** agencies report that owners care about CAC, ROAS and total cost per order — but
the *agency's own* survival metrics are MRR and days-to-cash.

## 02 · Real Estate (Karachi)
**Decision it drives:** price the stale listings, and chase the right enquiries.

| KPI | Why |
|---|---|
| Active listings | Live stock |
| Portfolio value | Asking value of everything on the books |
| Enquiries (30d) | Demand signal |
| Commission earned | Actual income (1.5–2.5% of sale) |
| Avg days on market | The one number that shows over-pricing |
| Avg commission / deal | Deal quality, not just deal count |

Charts: commission by month · enquiry sources (portal vs walk-in) · deal value by stage.

## 03 · Coaching & Courses
**Decision it drives:** whether the next cohort is worth running.

| KPI | Why |
|---|---|
| Active students | Book size |
| Collected revenue | Cash, with instalments accounted for |
| Attendance rate | Leading indicator of refunds |
| Avg fee / student | Pricing health |
| Outstanding fees | Instalments that stopped |
| Completion rate | The only honest marketing asset |

**Benchmark:** completion and attendance predict referrals more reliably than testimonials do.

---

## 04 · Retail Boutique / E-commerce
**Decision it drives:** buy more of what repeats, stop paying for one-time buyers.

| KPI | Why |
|---|---|
| Revenue | Net of cancellations and returns |
| Orders | Volume |
| Average order value | Basket health |
| Gross margin | Revenue minus COGS — profit before ads |
| Repeat customers | The cheapest revenue there is |
| Returns & refunds | Silent margin leak |

**Benchmarks:** global e-commerce conversion averages ~2.5–3.0% (Shopify-specific ~1.4–2.5%);
repeat purchase rate averages 25–30% with top performers at 40%+; AOV varies from ~$42
(food & beverage) to ~$275 (jewellery). Small margin, big volume — so margin belongs on screen.

---

## 05 · Restaurant & Café
**Decision it drives:** next week's rota and supplier order.

| KPI | Why |
|---|---|
| Revenue (30d) | Trailing trading reality |
| Average check | Covers × check = revenue; both move |
| Food cost % | Purchasing + portion control |
| Prime cost % | Food + labour — the number that decides profit |
| Delivery share | Margin differs by channel |
| Operating expenses | Everything above the line |

**Benchmarks:** prime cost 55–65% of sales (profitable operators 55–62%); food cost 28–35%,
industry average ~32%. Net margin for a full-service restaurant is only 3–8%, which is why
prime cost sits on the front screen and impressions never do.

## 06 · Salon · Spa · Aesthetics
**Decision it drives:** fill the empty chair before the client leaves the building.

| KPI | Why |
|---|---|
| Bookings (30d) | Volume |
| Revenue (30d) | Trailing performance |
| Average ticket | Upsell and add-on discipline |
| No-show & cancel | Pure lost revenue that can't be resold |
| Rebooking rate | Predictability of next month |
| Chair utilisation | Capacity you're already paying rent on |

**Benchmarks:** no-show rate 8–11% for salons; rebooking 60–80% for top performers;
chair utilisation 75–85%; median average ticket ~$114 (full-service).

## 07 · Gym · Fitness Studio
**Decision it drives:** stop the leak in the first 90 days.

| KPI | Why |
|---|---|
| Active members | Roster |
| MRR | Recurring revenue |
| Monthly churn | The leak |
| Visits / member / week | Engagement predicts churn |
| Renewals due (14d) | Revenue at risk, actionable today |
| Personal training | High-margin add-on |

**Benchmarks:** industry annual retention ~66.4% (HFA 2025), i.e. ~3.4% monthly churn; boutique
studios live at 2–3%; anything above 5–6% is a first-90-days problem.

## 08 · School · Academy
**Decision it drives:** who to call before the salary run.

| KPI | Why |
|---|---|
| Enrolled students | Capacity and staffing |
| Fee collected | Cash |
| Collection rate | Collected ÷ billed |
| Outstanding arrears | The actual chase list |
| Attendance rate | Parent satisfaction proxy |
| Avg monthly fee | Pricing/packaging |

**Benchmark:** healthy fee collection runs 90%+; below that, payroll timing gets tight.

## 09 · Clinic · Dental
**Decision it drives:** fill the chair, then collect for it.

| KPI | Why |
|---|---|
| New patients (30d) | Top of funnel |
| Appointments (30d) | Schedule utilisation |
| No-show rate | Unbillable lost time |
| Production (30d) | Completed treatment value |
| Collection rate | Production → cash |
| AR over 90 days | The billing process failing quietly |

**Benchmarks:** collections 98%+ of net production is best practice (95% average, <90% for two
months is a red flag); no-show 11–15% average with top practices under 5%; AR days under 30.

## 10 · Travel Agency
**Decision it drives:** which bookings to chase for deposit today.

| KPI | Why |
|---|---|
| Bookings (30d) | Volume |
| Gross booking value | Size of the book (not revenue) |
| Commission earned | Actual income |
| Average booking | Package mix |
| Cancellation rate | Refund exposure |

**Benchmark:** agency commission typically 8–15% of GMV — a big top line with thin margin, so

## 11 · Construction Contractor
**Decision it drives:** which job is about to lose money.

| KPI | Why |
|---|---|
| Active projects | Capacity |
| Contract backlog | Work sold but not delivered |
| Billed this month | Progress billing |
| Receivables | Cash stuck, including retention |
| Gross margin | Billed minus cost |
| Over-budget projects | Forecast cost above contract |

**Benchmarks:** general contractors average ~14.8% gross margin (CFMA), specialty trades 20–35%;
construction AR averages ~83 days including retainage, so retention must be visible separately.

## 12 · Event Photography
**Decision it drives:** sell the empty season, then deliver the backlog.

| KPI | Why |
|---|---|
| Events (30d) | Shoot volume |
| Revenue this year | Seasonality-aware revenue |
| Average package | Pricing health |
| Booked pipeline | Next season's safety |
| Pending deliveries | Editing backlog blocks new sales |
| Referral share | The studio's real marketing channel |

Seasonality note: a photography studio's monthly chart *should* look spiky — wedding season is

## 13 · SaaS / Software *(new)*
**Decision it drives:** whether growth is real or just new logos hiding churn.

| KPI | Why |
|---|---|
| MRR / ARR | Recurring revenue |
| Net revenue retention | Expansion vs churn on the SAME cohort |
| Logo churn (30d) | Customer leak |
| ARPA | Pricing and packaging |
| MRR growth (12m) | Trajectory |
| Open tickets + CSAT | Churn's leading indicator |

**Benchmarks:** median NRR ~97% for SMB (<$25K ACV), ~108% mid-market, ~118% enterprise; GRR
floor ~85–90%. **Important modelling detail:** NRR must be calculated on the customers who
existed 12 months ago — computing *total MRR now ÷ total MRR then* bakes new-customer growth
into "retention" and produces fantasy numbers like 150%.

---

## 14 · Logistics & Freight *(new)*
**Decision it drives:** which lane and carrier mix is destroying margin.

| KPI | Why |
|---|---|
| Shipments (30d) | Volume |
| On-time delivery (OTD/OTIF) | The promise customers pay for |
| Revenue (30d) + margin | Freight is a thin-margin volume business |
| Cost per shipment | Carrier/lane efficiency |
| Receivables | Freight bills age badly |
| Exceptions | Delays, damage, holds |

**Benchmarks:** OTIF 90–95% is good, 95%+ world-class, below 90% signals systemic problems.
Walmart's supplier standard is famously ~98%, which is why this sits front and centre.

---

## 15 · Law Firm *(new)*
**Decision it drives:** where the three-step leak is — worked, billed, collected.

| KPI | Why |
|---|---|
| Billable hours (30d) | Raw effort |
| Utilisation | Hours ÷ capacity |
| Realisation rate | Worked value → billed value |
| Collection rate | Billed → collected |
| WIP (unbilled) | Cash sitting in drawers |
| Receivables + lockup days | WIP days + AR days |

**Benchmarks (Clio Legal Trends 2025):** average utilisation 38% (≈3.0 billable hours in an
8-hour day), realisation 88%, collection 93%, median total lockup 93 days. Capacity-based
targets for fee earners are usually 65–75% of an 8-hour day — the gap between the two numbers
is exactly what makes this dashboard worth paying for.

# Part 2 — the fifteen specialist trades (v2)

Added in v2. Same rule: **six numbers, three charts, one table** — but the *units* are now
industry-specific, because a poultry farm does not think in rupees per month, it thinks in
feed-conversion ratio and rupees per kilogram of live weight.

| # | Industry | The unit that matters | Layout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16 | Boutique hotel | room-nights, ADR, RevPAR | hero |
| 17 | Plastics plant | OEE %, machine-minutes | gauge |
| 18 | Recruitment agency | days to fill, fee % of CTC | ledger |
| 19 | Community pharmacy | scripts/day, days of stock | calendar |
| 20 | Broiler farm | FCR, mortality %, Rs/kg live | scorecard |
| 21 | Fuel station | litres/day, Rs of dealer margin per litre | board |
| 22 | Managed IT (MSP) | minutes to first response, endpoints/tech | timeline |
| 23 | Auto workshop | job cards, bay-hours, parts margin | gauge |
| 24 | Printing press | impressions, waste sheets % | ledger |
| 25 | Garment unit | line efficiency %, DHU per 100 | scorecard |
| 26 | Bakery | kilograms baked, wastage by weight | calendar |
| 27 | Courier / last mile | on-time %, COD in float | timeline |
| 28 | Architecture studio | billable hours, fee per sqft | hero |
| 29 | Veterinary clinic | consults/day, vaccination uptake | grid (2-up) |
| 30 | Solar EPC | kW installed, Rs per watt | board |

## 16 · Boutique Hotel (44 rooms, Islamabad)
**Decision it drives:** which nights to push rate on, and which channel is buying the cheap rooms.

| KPI | Why it's there | Benchmark used |
|---|---|---|
| Occupancy · 30 days | Room-nights sold ÷ available; the denominator everyone forgets | US 2025: 62.3%; boutique 57–71% |
| ADR | Average daily rate across occupied rooms | US 2025: $160.54 |
| RevPAR | ADR × occupancy — the only number discounting cannot flatter | US 2025: $100.02 |
| Room revenue (MTD) | Cash view of the same week | — |
| Outstanding folios | City ledger only: checked-out stays carrying a balance | — |
| Average length of stay | Drives housekeeping and channel mix | 2.0–3.5 nights typical |

Charts: 12-month room revenue · booking channel mix · occupancy by month.
**Why the layout is different:** the occupancy number is the page, so it gets a 2×2 headline panel.

## 17 · Manufacturing Plant (injection moulding)
**Decision it drives:** which machine to stop apologising for and actually fix.

| KPI | Benchmark |
|---|---|
| OEE = Availability × Performance × Quality | World class 85%; most plants 40–65%; CNC/metal 50–70% |
| Availability | A ≥ 90% at world class |
| Performance | P ≥ 95% at world class |
| Quality (good ÷ total moulded) | Q ≥ 99.9% world class; scrap 1–3% average, >5% critical |
| Good output MTD | pieces, not rupees |
| Value past due | rupees of booked orders behind schedule |

Charts: daily good units · downtime by reason (machine-minutes) · OEE by line.
**Data detail:** each line carries its own health factor, so Line A reads 80%, Line C 65% — a plant
where every machine reports the same OEE is a plant with a fake dashboard.

## 18 · Recruitment Agency
**Decision it drives:** whether to keep taking roles from a client, and which consultant is behind.

| KPI | Benchmark |
|---|---|
| Open roles | live mandates |
| Fill rate | filled ÷ *concluded* mandates (filled + lost) — 70–80% is healthy |
| Time to fill | median 39 days non-executive; 45+ executive |
| Fees this month | placements × fee |
| Average fee % | contingency 15–25% of first-year CTC; retained 25–33% |
| Invoices overdue | the cash leak |

Charts: monthly fees · candidate sources · candidates by stage.

## 19 · Community Pharmacy
**Decision it drives:** what to reorder this week, and which stock is about to expire.

| KPI | Benchmark |
|---|---|
| Scripts per day | US average 217/day; 183 = break-even |
| Gross margin | 20.9% sector average (2025); warning below 17.5% |
| Stock cover (days) | target 25–35 days on hand |
| Lines below reorder | stockouts above ~2% are lost sales |
| Expiry risk (90 days) | value of stock expiring inside a quarter |
| OTC share | counter sales are the higher-margin half |

Charts: daily scripts · stock value by category · purchase value by distributor.

## 20 · Broiler Poultry Farm
**Decision it drives:** whether this cycle is making money before the feed bill arrives.

| KPI | Benchmark |
|---|---|
| FCR (kg feed ÷ kg live weight) | 1.5–1.7 good, <1.5 excellent, >1.8 poor |
| Mortality | 3–5% per cycle; elite <2% |
| Average live weight | 1.8–2.4 kg at 35–42 days |
| Birds in shed | live birds across active batches |
| Feed cost per kg gain | feed is ~66% of the cost of a bird |
| Realised rate per kg | Rs 330–520/kg by season |

Charts: daily feed intake · mortality by batch · sales by buyer.

## 21 · Fuel Station (retail pump)
**Decision it drives:** litres per nozzle-hour and how much of the day walked out on credit.

| KPI | Benchmark / note |
|---|---|
| Litres per day | volume is the only lever the dealer controls |
| Dealer margin Rs/L | **government-set: Rs 9.98/L** (from Sep 2026); OMC margin Rs 7.87/L, IFEM ~Rs 7.9/L |
| Fuel gross profit | litres × fixed margin |
| Shop sales | forecourt shop is the margin business (2–6% of turnover) |
| Credit outstanding | fleet accounts — the working-capital trap |
| Gross profit | fuel + shop contribution |

Charts: daily litres · product mix · shop sales by category.
**Why the layout is different:** a pump is run off a shift log, so the log *is* the page and the
KPI strip sits underneath it.
